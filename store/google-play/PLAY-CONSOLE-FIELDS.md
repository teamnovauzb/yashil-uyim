## Data safety — verified behavior and remaining hosting check

- No name, email, phone, account ID, payment, location, contact list, photo-library data, search text or saved-session selection is submitted by the app.
- Search and bookmarks are processed on-device. Public content is fetched with GET only; no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or push SDK is included.
- Requests use HTTPS. Supabase and media hosts can receive IP addresses, user-agent/request metadata and logs. Their actual retention, identifiers and processing purposes must be checked by the publisher before the top-level collection/sharing answer is finalized. Do not select “no data collected” solely because login is absent; do not claim logs are ephemeral without verification.
- If retained provider data falls within a listed data type, declare that actual type/purpose. Do not mark approximate location unless it is actually derived/used. Service-provider handling and user-initiated external links have their own rules; no advertising sale/sharing is implemented here.
- Account creation is absent. Local data can be cleared through Android settings; do not promise server-log deletion that the operator cannot perform. The policy provides a privacy contact, and the publisher must fill in a working support email.

The server/provider log settings were not accessed or changed. Data Safety remains a publisher declaration, not a form that can truthfully be auto-filled from frontend permissions alone.

## Reviewer notes — copy if a notes field is available

Yashil Uyim is a public ecological-festival companion. All functionality is accessible without registration, Telegram login, a phone number, a ticket, a payment or a test account. Open the five bottom tabs to browse the program, festival announcements, gallery and venue. Program items can be saved on the device; these bookmarks are not uploaded. Public content is currently read from the existing Supabase source. The information icon opens the app information screen and its in-app privacy policy. Gallery images/videos and the external venue map require connectivity. Cached program and announcement text can remain available offline. The app is primarily in Uzbek.
